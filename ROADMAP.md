# Roadmap

Where the game is, what is being built and what is only dreamed. **No dates are
promised** — a cultivator who rushes a breakthrough cripples the foundation.

The official game is live and keeps growing along this road; its status, and
what its players are promised if it ever has to close, are in the README's
[Project status](README.md#project-status).

## Done

- Realms, sub-layers, passive cultivation and meditation (light, deep, void,
  Dao); Qi, combat Qi and comprehension; the 3,000<!--#dao_laws--> Dao Laws and their fragments.
- Heavenly tribulations, breakthrough trials, foundation and core quality.
- One source of truth for power, stats and HP (`core/cultivator_soul.py`) that
  every screen reads.
- Techniques with Qi cost, mastery and scarcity (the sect libraries and their
  sealed shelves).
- Lifespan, prestige and rebirth.
- Turn-based combat, one engine for PvE and PvP; duels and the ranked arena;
  dungeons with four<!--#len:core/dungeon_quest_engine.py:DIFF_ORDER--> difficulties, floors and bosses; the world boss.
- NPC sects with ranks and libraries, player guilds with buildings, guild wars.
- Alchemy, forging, refining, enchanting; the spatial ring; spirit beasts and
  expeditions; the market and escrowed player trades.
- The living world: houses that remember who wronged them, regional states,
  five<!--#len:core/city_engine.py:CITY_META--> cities, caravans, celestial events, the sky's temperament.
- Quests that only exist when the world makes them true; achievements.
- Multiple worlds per server with per-world feature flags; the admin panel.
- The browser client — phone and desktop — and the Telegram Mini App, in three<!--#languages-->
  languages.
- The community edition: the game without Telegram in one command, the
  documentation of every system (`docs/`), continuous integration on every pull
  request, issue and pull-request templates.

## In progress

- **The game stops being text.** Screen by screen, the client becomes a scene:
  the body, the ring, the beasts, the sect, the guild and the dungeons already
  "light up"; the workshop (alchemy and forge) and the rest of the world are
  next.

## Dreamed

- Reincarnation that carries comprehension into the next life.
- A beast arena.
- Auctions.
- More of the world's regions, opened by the people who explore them.

Have an idea? Open an issue — tell us the story the mechanic tells, not only
the numbers.
