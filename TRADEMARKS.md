# The Nivrael name

The code of this project is free software ([AGPL-3.0-only](LICENSE)) and its
art and text are under [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](LICENSE-ASSETS). **The name is a
different matter.**

"**Nivrael**", "**Nivrael — Caminho da Imortalidade**", its logo and the
identity of the official game (its domains, its Telegram bot and channels, its
worlds and community) are **reserved for the official game**. The licences above
cover the code and the assets; they do not grant any right to the name.

## What you can do

- Run this code for yourself, your friends or your class, privately, under any
  name — including "Nivrael" — as long as you do not present it to others as the
  official game.
- Say that your project is "**based on Nivrael**", "a **fork of Nivrael**" or
  "built with the Nivrael community edition".
- Link to the official game and to this repository.
- Write about Nivrael, review it, make videos and guides.

## What you cannot do

- Publish or operate a game, a server, an app, a bot or a website **called
  Nivrael** (or a confusingly similar name), or use its logo, if it is not the
  official game.
- Suggest that your fork is endorsed by, affiliated with or a continuation of
  the official game, or move its players to your instance under its name.
- Use the name in a domain, store listing, bot name or social account.

A public fork therefore needs **its own name and its own logo** — and, under the
art licence, to keep the attribution and the same licence for the art it reuses.
