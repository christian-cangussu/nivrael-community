## Who decides what

- **Every pull request** is reviewed and merged by a maintainer, after the
  continuous integration is green. The `main` branch is protected: nothing
  reaches it without a pull request.
- **The official game's servers** are decided by its owner alone. A merge here
  never deploys anything by itself; the owner chooses which version goes to
  the official game, and when.
- **Security reports** go to the maintainers privately — see
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Becoming a maintainer

By invitation, after a history of accepted pull requests and of helping other
contributors in issues and reviews. A maintainer reviews and merges pull
requests here.

No maintainer — ever, whatever their role — receives access to the official
game's server, its credentials or its players' data. Those stay with the
owner of the official game.
